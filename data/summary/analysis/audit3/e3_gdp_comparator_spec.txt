E3  log GDP per capita plus region dummies, as the aggregate comparator that
    needs no survey data from other countries.

STATUS: blocked. benchmark_v13.py --gdp is coded but was never run because the
input file does not exist. This is the only comparator that speaks to a
researcher holding no survey data at all, which is the silicon-sampling use
case, so the gap is currently acknowledged in the memo rather than closed.

REQUIRED INPUT: gdp_per_capita.csv, two columns, thirty rows.
    cntry   ISO-2 code, matching country_bloc_assignment.csv exactly
    gdp_pc  GDP per capita, current US dollars, one reference year

Round 11 fieldwork ran across 2023 and 2024, so use a single year and record
it. World Bank NY.GDP.PCAP.CD covers twenty-nine of the thirty; Montenegro is
present, so no substitution is needed. Do not mix sources across countries.

SPECIFICATION once the file exists, per item:
    regress the human country mean on log(gdp_pc) and bloc dummies, thirty
    observations, leave-one-out predicted values; report r against the human
    mean and range-normalised MAE, on the same two axes as the model and the
    regional average. Leave-one-out is required for comparability with the
    regional arm; with five blocs and one covariate the in-sample fit would
    otherwise carry six of thirty degrees of freedom.

REPORTING: this comparator does not need survey data from other countries, so
it is the only row of the benchmark table exempt from the mandatory caveat.
